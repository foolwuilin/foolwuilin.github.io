There are places you visit and places you *answer*. Times Square is pure noise — screens, traffic, a thousand languages at once. So I did the only thing that made sense to me: I pulled out the portable, dropped the needle, and added one more voice to the chaos.

This is what "Scratch the World" means. A tourist takes a photo to hold the moment. I take a scratch. The cut I laid down here will always sound like New Year's in New York — cold hands, bright lights, and that one baby scratch that cut clean through all of it.

If you're learning, this is the lesson: you don't need a club or a crowd. You need a record, a fader, and somewhere that means something to you.

**Answer first:** Portablism is scratching and cutting using *portable* turntables instead of full-size decks. It strips the DJ setup down to the essentials so you can practice — and perform — anywhere.

Portablism grew out of a simple desire: take turntablism with you. Instead of two heavy decks and a mixer, you carry a small turntable and a portable fader. It's one of the cheapest ways into scratching, and it's why I can scratch on a street corner, a rooftop, or in Times Square.

It's not a lesser version of turntablism — it's turntablism set free from the booth.

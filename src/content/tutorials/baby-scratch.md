**Answer first:** A baby scratch is a two-note move — you push the record forward and pull it back with the crossfader open the entire time. No fader cuts, just your record hand. It is the building block for every other scratch.

## Steps

1. Find a sound with a sharp start (a vocal "ahh" or a drum hit works well).
2. Keep the crossfader open (audio passing through) the whole time.
3. Push the record forward about an inch, then pull it back to the start. That's one baby scratch.
4. Keep the motion even — same distance forward and back, steady speed.
5. Once it's smooth, speed it up and play it in time with a beat.

## Why it matters

Every advanced scratch (the scribble, the chirp, the flare) is built on the muscle memory you develop here. Spend real time on it before moving on.

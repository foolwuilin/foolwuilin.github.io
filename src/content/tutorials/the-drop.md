**Answer first:** The drop (also called a release) is about precision — you "drop" the record so a sound starts at an exact point, with the fader open. It trains the record-hand control that every scratch depends on.

## Steps

1. Pick your sound and find where it begins on the record.
2. Hold the record still just before the sound starts.
3. Release cleanly so the sound plays forward naturally, fader open.
4. Catch it and reset to the same start point.
5. Repeat until you can hit the exact start every time, in rhythm.

## Why it matters

Precision is everything in scratching. The drop builds the control you'll use to place sounds musically, not randomly.
